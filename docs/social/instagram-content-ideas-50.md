# 50 Instagram Content Ideas

Every idea below is built for **sends** first, saves second. Each carries:

- **Hook** — the literal first line. Say it exactly as written; the hook is the post.
- **Angle** — the payload that earns the hook.
- **Format** / **Pillar** — resolves to `instagram-format-playbook.md` and the
  pillar system in `instagram-growth-strategy.md`.
- **Signal** — the one metric this post is engineered to move.
- **Link** — the `/links` destination, where one exists in the library.

**⚠️ Numbers marked `[your number]`** need a real figure from your Stripe,
GA4, Beehiiv, or ship·able cohort data before you shoot. Do not invent them —
the entire positioning advantage of this account is that our numbers are
defensible. If you can't source it, cut the beat.

## MISTAKES (7)
*The send driver here is recognition. People send these to someone they've
watched make the mistake.*

**1. The 61-idea Notion doc**
- **Hook:** "Your ideas doc isn't a pipeline. It's a graveyard with better fonts."
- **Angle:** Every idea you add lowers the odds you ship any of them. Adding an idea feels like progress and costs nothing, so the doc grows forever. The fix is a hard rule: the doc caps at 5. Idea 6 kills the weakest one. Constraint forces the decision you've been avoiding.
- **Format:** `founder-pov` · **Pillar:** MIRROR · **Signal:** follows
- **Link:** `/articles/stop-overthinking-start-building`

**2. Building the settings page first**
- **Hook:** "You built a settings page for an app with zero users."
- **Angle:** Designers over-build the parts they know how to build. Auth, settings, dark mode, an empty state with a nice illustration. All of it is procrastination wearing a Figma file. Screen-record the three screens that actually matter and strike out everything else.
- **Format:** `three-screen-mvp` · **Pillar:** PROOF · **Signal:** saves
- **Link:** `/articles/the-3-screen-mvp-framework`

**3. Picking the idea you can't reach**
- **Hook:** "Great idea. Now name the exact place your first 10 customers already hang out."
- **Angle:** Most weekend MVPs die on distribution, not build. If you can't name a subreddit, a Slack, a directory, or a Facebook group where 500 of these people already are, the idea is unbuildable regardless of how good it is. Do this test *before* you open Cursor.
- **Format:** `honest-critique` · **Pillar:** VERDICT · **Signal:** sends
- **Link:** `/articles/how-to-get-your-first-10-customers`

**4. Charging $9 because you're scared**
- **Hook:** "You priced it at $9 because you were scared, and now you need 500 customers."
- **Angle:** Underpricing isn't humility, it's a customer-count problem. $9 × 500 vs $49 × 90 — same revenue, wildly different amount of support, marketing, and luck required. Show both columns on screen. The cheap price is the harder business.
- **Format:** `revenue-breakdown` · **Pillar:** PROOF · **Signal:** saves + sends
- **Link:** `/articles/how-to-price-your-weekend-mvp`

**5. Asking friends if they'd use it**
- **Hook:** "'Would you use this?' is the most expensive question in startups."
- **Angle:** Everyone says yes. Yes costs them nothing. The Mom Test rewrite: ask what they did last time they had the problem, what it cost them, and what they're using now. Past behaviour over future intention — every single time.
- **Format:** `honest-critique` · **Pillar:** VERDICT · **Signal:** sends
- **Link:** `/articles/how-to-talk-to-customers-mom-test`

**6. Shipping what the AI wrote without reading it**
- **Hook:** "Your AI shipped an API key to production and you clicked accept."
- **Angle:** Name the specific things vibe-coded apps leak — keys in client bundles, unprotected endpoints, missing row-level auth. Show one real diff on screen. This is the highest-trust post on the list because nobody in this niche wants to say it.
- **Format:** `workflow-demo` · **Pillar:** CRAFT · **Signal:** saves
- **Link:** `/articles/vibe-coding-security-for-founders`

**7. The domain you bought and never used**
- **Hook:** "How many domains do you own for products that don't exist?"
- **Angle:** Buying the domain is the cheapest possible simulation of starting. It gives the dopamine of commitment with none of the risk. Ask for the number in the comments — this is a one-token comment ask, which is exactly what this audience will actually do.
- **Format:** `founder-pov` · **Pillar:** MIRROR · **Signal:** comments

## MYTHS (6)
*Myth-busting is inherently sendable — it settles an argument the viewer is
already having.*

**8. "You need to learn to code first"**
- **Hook:** "You don't need to learn to code. You need to learn to scope."
- **Angle:** The people shipping in 2026 aren't better engineers, they're better at cutting. Show the same idea scoped three ways: 6 months, 3 weeks, one weekend. The weekend version is the only one that exists.
- **Format:** `contrarian-product` · **Pillar:** VERDICT · **Signal:** sends
- **Link:** `/articles/how-to-build-your-first-app-in-a-weekend`

**9. "AI wrappers are dead"**
- **Hook:** "'AI wrappers are dead' is something people say instead of shipping."
- **Angle:** Steelman it properly first — thin wrappers over a chat box genuinely do die. Then break it: the wrapper isn't the product, the *workflow and the data you own* is. Name two specific wrappers with real revenue and what they own that the model doesn't.
- **Format:** `honest-critique` · **Pillar:** VERDICT · **Signal:** sends
- **Link:** `/articles/is-it-too-late-to-build-an-ai-wrapper`

**10. "The market is too crowded"**
- **Hook:** "Crowded means people pay. Empty means nobody wants it."
- **Angle:** Every first-time builder wants an empty market. Empty markets are empty for a reason. Take a crowded category, show three incumbents' pricing pages, then carve one audience none of them serve properly.
- **Format:** `niche-the-incumbent` · **Pillar:** VERDICT · **Signal:** sends

**11. "You need a big idea"**
- **Hook:** "A $1,000/month product changes your life. A billion-dollar idea changes nothing, because you won't build it."
- **Angle:** Do the math on screen: $1k/mo is 34 customers at $29. Thirty-four people. You know thirty-four people. Scale the ambition down until it becomes reachable, because reachable is the only kind that gets built.
- **Format:** `revenue-breakdown` · **Pillar:** PROOF · **Signal:** saves
- **Link:** `/articles/the-1k-month-idea`

**12. "Launch when it's ready"**
- **Hook:** "It will never be ready. That's not a bug in your product, it's a bug in the sentence."
- **Angle:** "Ready" is unfalsifiable — there's no test that returns true. Replace it with a date and a definition: one deployed URL that does one thing for one person. Ready is a feeling; deployed is a fact.
- **Format:** `founder-pov` · **Pillar:** MIRROR · **Signal:** follows

**13. "You need an audience first"**
- **Hook:** "Build the audience first' is advice from people who already have one."
- **Angle:** Your first 10 customers do not come from an audience. They come from 40 direct messages to people who have the problem. Show the actual message template on screen — the whole post is the template.
- **Format:** `workflow-demo` · **Pillar:** CRAFT · **Signal:** saves
- **Link:** `/articles/how-to-get-your-first-10-customers`

## UNPOPULAR OPINIONS (7)
*These are the send engine. Each one has a cost — it names something
specific as wrong.*

**14. Boring is a moat**
- **Hook:** "The best business on this list is a tool for contractors who still fax things."
- **Angle:** Nobody wants to build for zoning boards, recall notices, or brake shops. That's the entire opportunity — no competition, urgent pain, customers who pay invoices without a trial period. Glamour is a tax.
- **Format:** `boring-business` · **Pillar:** PROOF · **Signal:** sends
- **Link:** `/ideas/ai-zoning-intelligence`

**15. Most "validation" is procrastination**
- **Hook:** "You didn't validate the idea. You spent three weeks avoiding building it."
- **Angle:** There's a real 48-hour version of validation — a landing page, 40 DMs, and a payment link. Everything beyond that is research theatre. Draw the line clearly, on screen, between the two.
- **Format:** `honest-critique` · **Pillar:** VERDICT · **Signal:** sends
- **Link:** `/articles/validate-startup-idea-48-hours`

**16. Stop building for founders**
- **Hook:** "Everyone builds for founders because founders are the only people they've met."
- **Angle:** Founders are broke, churn fast, and build it themselves. Meanwhile freelancers, recruiters, trainers, and property managers pay for years and never consider building anything. Name three categories with better economics.
- **Format:** `contrarian-product` · **Pillar:** VERDICT · **Signal:** comments

**17. Your design skill is slowing you down**
- **Hook:** "Being good at design is why you haven't shipped."
- **Angle:** The most uncomfortable post you can make for your own audience. You can see the polished version, so the ugly v1 feels like a personal failure — so you keep polishing something with zero users. Every designer watching will feel caught. That's why they'll send it.
- **Format:** `founder-pov` · **Pillar:** MIRROR · **Signal:** sends + follows

**18. Free tiers kill weekend MVPs**
- **Hook:** "Your free tier isn't generous. It's how you avoid finding out nobody will pay."
- **Angle:** A free tier means the money question is deferred indefinitely, and the money question is the only one that matters at this stage. Charge from day one, even $9, even to five people. Five payers beats 500 free users as information.
- **Format:** `contrarian-product` · **Pillar:** VERDICT · **Signal:** comments

**19. Build in public is mostly performance now**
- **Hook:** "Building in public turned into posting in public."
- **Angle:** Be fair to it — it genuinely works when there's a shipped thing attached. It's dead weight when the content *is* the product. The test: if you stopped posting, would anything you built still exist?
- **Format:** `honest-critique` · **Pillar:** VERDICT · **Signal:** sends
- **Link:** `/articles/build-in-public-2026`

**20. Don't quit your job**
- **Hook:** "Quitting your job to build is the most expensive way to buy focus."
- **Angle:** Runway pressure makes you pick the safe idea, price it too low, and take the consulting gig by month four. Name the actual signals that mean it's time — revenue, not enthusiasm.
- **Format:** `founder-pov` · **Pillar:** MIRROR · **Signal:** follows + sends
- **Link:** `/articles/when-to-quit-your-job`

## LESSONS (6)
*First person, specific, and only credible with real detail. Use `[your
number]` placeholders honestly.*

**21. What [your number] people did on a live call**
- **Hook:** "I watched [your number] people try to ship something in 90 minutes. [X] of them did."
- **Angle:** The pattern in the ones who shipped: they cut the idea in the first 10 minutes. The ones who didn't were still choosing at minute 40. Deciding is the skill, not building.
- **Format:** `founder-pov` · **Pillar:** RECEIPTS · **Signal:** follows + link clicks
- **Link:** `/shipable` · **Requires:** real ship·able cohort data

**22. The idea I published that I'd now kill**
- **Hook:** "I published this idea. I was wrong about it. Here's the part I got wrong."
- **Angle:** Take one of the 130 published ideas and critique it honestly — the distribution assumption that doesn't hold, or the incumbent that closed the gap. **Run this once a month.** It is the single strongest trust move available to this account and no competitor will copy it.
- **Format:** `honest-critique` · **Pillar:** VERDICT · **Signal:** sends

**23. 130 ideas, one pattern**
- **Hook:** "I've researched 130 startup ideas. The ones that work all share one boring trait."
- **Angle:** The winners have a customer who *already pays someone* for a worse version. Not a new behaviour — a redirected budget. Show three from the library where the existing spend is visible.
- **Format:** `business-model-map` · **Pillar:** PROOF · **Signal:** saves + sends
- **Link:** `/startup-ideas`

**24. The article that outperformed everything**
- **Hook:** "The post that got the most traffic wasn't the clever one."
- **Angle:** Show the real GA4 curve. The winner is almost always the most boringly specific piece. Lesson transfers directly: specific beats clever, in content and in products.
- **Format:** `founder-pov` · **Pillar:** MIRROR · **Signal:** follows
- **Requires:** real GA4 data

**25. What I'd rebuild differently**
- **Hook:** "I spent [X] weeks on the part nobody uses."
- **Angle:** Point at a real feature on your own site with near-zero engagement. Then the redirect: the thing you almost skipped is the thing people actually came for. Vulnerable, specific, and it teaches scoping without lecturing.
- **Format:** `founder-pov` · **Pillar:** MIRROR · **Signal:** sends
- **Requires:** real analytics

**26. The first 10 customers took 40 DMs**
- **Hook:** "Ten customers. Forty messages. Zero audience."
- **Angle:** Show the funnel honestly — sent, replied, called, paid. The unglamorous arithmetic is the whole point, and it's the antidote to "I need an audience first."
- **Format:** `revenue-breakdown` · **Pillar:** PROOF · **Signal:** saves
- **Link:** `/articles/how-to-get-your-first-10-customers`

## FRAMEWORKS (6)
*Highest save rate of any category. All six are carousel-first.*

**27. The 3-Screen Test**
- **Hook:** "If you can't draw it in three screens, you're not building a product. You're building a company."
- **Angle:** Input → transform → output. Take something that sounds enormous and cut it live. Slide 6 carries the payoff, slide 7 extends it, slide 8 converts.
- **Format:** carousel (`pipeline-8slide`) · **Pillar:** PROOF · **Signal:** saves
- **Link:** `/articles/the-3-screen-mvp-framework`

**28. The 48-Hour Validation Loop**
- **Hook:** "Two days to know if it's real. Not two months."
- **Angle:** Friday night: scope + landing page. Saturday: 40 DMs. Sunday: payment link. Each stage has a kill criterion — the kill criteria are what make it a framework rather than a checklist.
- **Format:** carousel (`pipeline-8slide`) · **Pillar:** PROOF · **Signal:** saves
- **Link:** `/articles/validate-startup-idea-48-hours`

**29. The Redirected Budget Test**
- **Hook:** "Four questions. If you answer no to any of them, don't build it."
- **Angle:** Who has this problem weekly? What do they pay for it now? Where are 500 of them? Would they pay before it's finished? Four gates, no scoring rubric — binary, so it forces a decision instead of a spreadsheet.
- **Format:** carousel (`wmv-default-8slide`) · **Pillar:** PROOF · **Signal:** saves + sends

**30. The Scope Knife**
- **Hook:** "Cut every feature that isn't the reason someone would pay."
- **Angle:** Run it on a real idea from the library, on camera. Eleven features on screen, nine struck through. The visual of the strikethrough is the shareable asset.
- **Format:** `three-screen-mvp` · **Pillar:** PROOF · **Signal:** saves

**31. The Price Ladder**
- **Hook:** "$9, $29, $99 — the number changes who you have to become."
- **Angle:** Each price implies a customer count, a support load, and a marketing channel. Show all three columns. Choosing a price is choosing a business model, and most people choose accidentally.
- **Format:** carousel (`stack-grid-8slide`) · **Pillar:** PROOF · **Signal:** saves
- **Link:** `/articles/how-to-price-your-weekend-mvp`

**32. The Weekend Stack**
- **Hook:** "Six tools. One weekend. Nothing else allowed."
- **Angle:** Opinionated and closed — Claude Code or Cursor, Bolt or v0, Vercel, Convex or Supabase, Stripe, Beehiiv. The constraint *is* the framework. The comments will fight about the picks, which is the point.
- **Format:** carousel (`stack-grid-8slide`) · **Pillar:** CRAFT · **Signal:** saves + comments
- **Link:** `/articles/non-technical-founder-stack-2026`

## HIDDEN OPPORTUNITIES (6)
*Your unfair advantage — 130 researched ideas nobody else has.*

**33. One problem, four businesses**
- **Hook:** "One problem. Four businesses. Pick your risk tolerance."
- **Angle:** Take a single pain and map tool / marketplace / service / data across it, each with a price and a difficulty rating. Then take a position on which one you'd build. Highest value per minute of production on this entire list.
- **Format:** `business-model-map` · **Pillar:** PROOF · **Signal:** saves + sends

**34. The industries that still run on WhatsApp**
- **Hook:** "Tour operators run million-dollar businesses out of a WhatsApp inbox."
- **Angle:** Whole industries coordinate in group chats with no system of record. Every one of those chats is a product. Name three, with what breaks in each.
- **Format:** `boring-business` · **Pillar:** PROOF · **Signal:** sends
- **Link:** `/ideas/whatsapp-tour-guide-comms`

**35. Compliance deadlines are free distribution**
- **Hook:** "There's a date on the calendar that forces thousands of businesses to buy something."
- **Angle:** ADA scans, tax quarters, recall notices, safety certifications. A deadline does your marketing for you and makes urgency real without a fake countdown timer.
- **Format:** `idea-breakdown` · **Pillar:** PROOF · **Signal:** reach
- **Link:** `/ideas/website-accessibility-ada-scanner`

**36. The freelancer tax**
- **Hook:** "Freelancers lose [your number] hours a month to work they never agreed to do."
- **Angle:** Scope creep, chasing invoices, proving income for a mortgage. Each is a product nobody's built properly because founders don't build for freelancers. Show all three, one screen each.
- **Format:** `business-model-map` · **Pillar:** PROOF · **Signal:** sends
- **Link:** `/ideas/freelance-scope-creep-detector` · **Requires:** a sourced stat

**37. Build for the audience you already are**
- **Hook:** "You're a designer. Build the thing that makes your Tuesday less annoying."
- **Angle:** The best first product is one where you're the customer, because customer research collapses to introspection and your first 10 customers are already in your DMs. Show three designer-shaped products.
- **Format:** `founder-pov` · **Pillar:** MIRROR · **Signal:** follows

**38. The incumbent's ignored segment**
- **Hook:** "[Big tool] has millions of users and serves none of them well."
- **Angle:** Pick a genuinely huge tool. Show the specific way generality fails one narrow user. Carve that user out. You don't beat them — you leave them.
- **Format:** `niche-the-incumbent` · **Pillar:** VERDICT · **Signal:** sends

## TRENDS (6)
*Time-sensitive. Shoot these within days of the news, or don't shoot them.*

**39. The agent gold rush is mostly demos**
- **Hook:** "Ninety percent of AI agent demos would fall over on a real customer's data."
- **Angle:** Be fair — agents genuinely work in narrow, bounded workflows. They fall apart in open-ended ones. Draw the line, then show what a *shippable* agent scope actually looks like.
- **Format:** `honest-critique` · **Pillar:** VERDICT · **Signal:** sends
- **Link:** `/articles/build-an-ai-agent-app-in-a-weekend`

**40. Sell the outcome, not the seat**
- **Hook:** "Stop selling AI subscriptions. Start selling the finished work."
- **Angle:** The per-seat SaaS model is a poor fit for AI-delivered work. Charge per proposal, per report, per audit. Show both pricing pages side by side.
- **Format:** `contrarian-product` · **Pillar:** VERDICT · **Signal:** comments
- **Link:** `/articles/stop-selling-ai-subscriptions-start-selling-ai-work`

**41. AI search is the new distribution**
- **Hook:** "Your customers are asking ChatGPT for a tool like yours right now. It doesn't know you exist."
- **Angle:** AEO in practice — structured data, question-shaped headings, real citations. Show a live query where a competitor gets named and you don't. The screen recording is the argument.
- **Format:** `workflow-demo` · **Pillar:** CRAFT · **Signal:** saves
- **Link:** `/articles/get-cited-by-chatgpt-and-perplexity`

**42. What the new models actually change**
- **Hook:** "The new model doesn't change what you should build. It changes how long it takes."
- **Angle:** Ship within a week of any major model release. The take is deliberately anti-hype — capability moved, the bottleneck didn't. The bottleneck is still that you haven't decided.
- **Format:** `founder-pov` · **Pillar:** MIRROR · **Signal:** reach + follows
- **Link:** `/articles/anthropic-fable-5-for-builders`

**43. The real cost of running an AI app**
- **Hook:** "This app costs $[your number] a month to run. Here's the line-item breakdown."
- **Angle:** API + hosting + DB, itemised on screen. Almost nobody publishes this, which is exactly why it gets sent. Trust is built in the unglamorous numbers.
- **Format:** `revenue-breakdown` · **Pillar:** PROOF · **Signal:** saves + sends
- **Link:** `/articles/what-it-costs-to-run-an-ai-app`

**44. Cursor vs Claude Code vs Lovable**
- **Hook:** "You're not choosing a tool. You're choosing how much you want to understand."
- **Angle:** Reframe the comparison away from features toward the trade-off nobody names: speed now vs ability to fix it later. Take a position at the end — comparisons without a verdict get scrolled.
- **Format:** carousel (`stack-grid-8slide`) · **Pillar:** CRAFT · **Signal:** saves + comments
- **Link:** `/articles/cursor-vs-claude-code-vs-lovable-2026`

**45. Micro-SaaS is eating agency work**
- **Hook:** "The thing you do manually for clients is a product. You just haven't priced it."
- **Angle:** Freelancers and agencies sit on productisable workflows and keep selling hours. Show the conversion: one repeated deliverable → one tool → recurring revenue.
- **Format:** `idea-breakdown` · **Pillar:** PROOF · **Signal:** reach

## PAIN POINTS (5)
*Highest emotional charge on the list. These are the follow engine — shoot
them face-to-camera.*

**46. The 14-month idea**
- **Hook:** "You've had the same idea for fourteen months. Someone else shipped it in June."
- **Angle:** Not a scolding — a diagnosis. You didn't lose to a better builder, you lost to someone with a smaller version of the same idea. End with one thing to do tonight, small enough to actually do.
- **Format:** `founder-pov` · **Pillar:** MIRROR · **Signal:** follows + sends

**47. The 47 tabs**
- **Hook:** "Forty-seven tabs open. Zero files created."
- **Angle:** Research is the socially acceptable form of avoidance — it looks like work, it feels like progress, it produces nothing. Name the moment the tabs became the project.
- **Format:** `founder-pov` · **Pillar:** MIRROR · **Signal:** follows

**48. Explaining it at dinner again**
- **Hook:** "Your partner has heard about this idea for a year and has stopped asking."
- **Angle:** The quiet social cost of not shipping. This is the most emotionally exposed post on the list, which is exactly why it will outperform. One line of relief at the end — a URL, however small, ends the conversation.
- **Format:** `founder-pov` · **Pillar:** MIRROR · **Signal:** sends + follows

**49. Watching someone worse than you ship**
- **Hook:** "Someone with half your taste just launched the thing you've been designing in your head."
- **Angle:** Sit in it for a beat before resolving. Taste is not the constraint — taste is *why* you're stuck, because you can see the gap between v1 and the version in your head. The people shipping have simply agreed to be embarrassed for two weeks.
- **Format:** `founder-pov` · **Pillar:** MIRROR · **Signal:** sends

**50. Sunday night**
- **Hook:** "It's Sunday. You said this was the weekend."
- **Angle:** Post Sunday evening, when the feeling is live. Two paths from here: another Notion entry, or one deployed URL by Tuesday. Show the smallest possible version of their idea — the one that could be live in 90 minutes.
- **Format:** `founder-pov` · **Pillar:** MIRROR · **Signal:** follows + link clicks
- **Link:** `/shipable`

carousel within 14 days and boosted for profile visits. Winners get rebuilt,
not retired.
