# Instagram Format Playbook

Beat-by-beat structure for every format used in
`content/social/reels/campaigns/*/calendar.csv`. Until now these formats
existed only as column values with no shared definition — which is why
output quality drifts between weeks.

**Rule:** the `format` column in a calendar row must resolve to a section in
this file. No undocumented formats.

---

## 0. Universal rules

Apply to every Reel regardless of format.

**Length:** 22–34s. Under 22s reads thin; over 34s collapses completion for
this persona. `workflow-demo` may run to 45s — only if the screen recording
carries it.

**The first 1.2 seconds** must contain all three:
1. A visual that isn't a talking head (a screen, a number, a document)
2. A spoken claim or question — never "hey guys" and never the product name
3. On-screen text carrying the searchable phrase

**Banned openers:** "Let me tell you about…", "This startup idea is…", any
logo sting, any 3-2-1 countdown, the product name.

**The loop-close:** the final line rhymes conceptually with the first so a
replay is coherent. Replays are watch time and watch time is the gate.

**On-screen identity marker** at the loop point (not the start) — a small
`weekendmvp` mark in the last 3s. It travels with sends and drives profile
visits without costing the opening frame.

**Caption structure:**
```
[Line 1: searchable phrase, ≤40 chars, no emoji]

[2–4 lines: the specific numbers the Reel didn't have room for]

[CTA: one action, phrased as a role, not a favour]

[3–5 tags: 2 broad, 2 niche, 1 branded]
```

**CTA library** (never "tag a friend," never "double tap"):
| Goal | CTA |
|---|---|
| Sends | "Send this to the person who keeps saying they'll build something." |
| Comments | "A or B — one word." |
| Saves | "Save this; you'll need the numbers when you scope it." |
| Follows | "I break down one of these every day." |
| Link | "Full research + build path in the link." |
| DM | "DM me SHIP and I'll send the sheet." |

## 1. `idea-breakdown` — PROOF
**Job:** reach · **Target:** 60% completion, 1.0% sends · **Length:** 28–34s

The workhorse. One idea from the library, fully argued in 30 seconds.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3s | The **pain**, as a number or a scene. "Freelancers lose 12 hours a month to work they never agreed to." Never the product name. |
| Stakes | 3–8s | Who has this, how often, what it costs them. One real stat. |
| Turn | 8–14s | "Someone's already paying $X for a worse version of this" — name the incumbent. |
| Build | 14–22s | The 3-screen scope. Concrete: input → transform → output. Tool named. |
| Money | 22–28s | Price × customers = the number. Small and believable. $1k–5k/mo, not $1M. |
| Loop | 28–32s | Callback to the hook + "this is a weekend build." |

**Fails when:** it becomes a feature list. If there's no named competitor and
no number, it's an idea-dump post and it will underperform.

---

## 2. `three-screen-mvp` — PROOF
**Job:** saves · **Target:** 3% saves/reach · **Length:** 25–30s

Scope reduction as spectacle. Take something that sounds like a company and
cut it to three screens on camera.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3s | "This sounds like a 6-month build. It's three screens." |
| Over-scope | 3–9s | Show the bloated version — 11 features on screen, then strike them out. |
| Screen 1 | 9–14s | The input. What the user pastes/uploads/types. |
| Screen 2 | 14–19s | The transform. Where the AI call lives. One sentence. |
| Screen 3 | 19–25s | The output. What they'd screenshot and send to their boss. |
| Loop | 25–29s | "Everything else is v2." |

**Fails when:** the three screens are abstract. They must be drawable.

---

## 3. `revenue-breakdown` — PROOF
**Job:** saves + link clicks · **Target:** 3% saves · **Length:** 28–34s

The math post. This is the format that most separates us from idea-dump
accounts, because the numbers are defensible.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3s | The end number first. "$4,200 a month from 84 customers." |
| Price | 3–9s | Why this price. Anchor to what they already pay for something worse. |
| Customers | 9–16s | Where the first 10 come from — a *specific* place (a subreddit, a directory, a Slack). |
| Cost | 16–24s | Real running cost. API + hosting. This is the trust beat — nobody else shows it. |
| Margin | 24–30s | What's left. Honest. |
| Loop | 30–33s | "Small numbers, real money." |

**Fails when:** the customer-acquisition beat is hand-waved. "Just post on
Twitter" destroys the credibility the rest of the post built.

---

## 4. `honest-critique` — VERDICT ⭐ send engine
**Job:** sends + comments · **Target:** 1.8% sends/reach · **Length:** 25–32s

The highest-leverage format in the system. Take an idea — ideally a popular
one, occasionally one of our own — and say why it doesn't work.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3s | The verdict, stated flatly. "This idea gets posted every week. It doesn't work." |
| Steelman | 3–9s | Why it's appealing. Be genuinely fair — this is what makes it sendable rather than cheap. |
| The break | 9–18s | The specific mechanism of failure. Distribution, not tech, 80% of the time. |
| Salvage | 18–26s | What *would* work — the narrowed version. Never end on pure negativity. |
| Loop | 26–30s | "The idea isn't wrong. The scope is." |

**Fails when:** it's contrarian without a mechanism. This audience can tell
the difference between an argument and engagement bait in about two seconds.

**Note:** critiquing one of our own published ideas 1× per month is the single
strongest trust move available to this account.

---

## 5. `contrarian-product` — VERDICT
**Job:** comments + discussion · **Target:** 40+ comments · **Length:** 22–28s

A take on the *category* rather than an idea. "AI wrappers are fine, actually."
"Stop building for founders." "Your niche isn't too small — it's too vague."

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3s | The take, unhedged. |
| Consensus | 3–8s | What everyone says. Name it precisely. |
| Evidence | 8–18s | Two concrete counter-examples with numbers. |
| Ask | 18–25s | A binary question. "Wrapper or platform — which are you building?" |

**Fails when:** the take is safe. If nobody could reasonably disagree, it
won't get comments.

---

## 6. `niche-the-incumbent` — VERDICT
**Job:** sends · **Length:** 25–32s

Take a big tool everyone knows and carve one audience out of it.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3s | "[Big tool] has 40 million users and serves none of them well." |
| Bloat | 3–10s | The specific way generality fails a specific user. |
| Carve | 10–18s | The one audience. Named, narrow, reachable. |
| Proof | 18–26s | Why they'd switch and what they'd pay. |
| Loop | 26–30s | "You don't beat them. You leave them." |

---

## 7. `founder-pov` — MIRROR ⭐ follow engine
**Job:** follows · **Target:** 8 follows/1k reach · **Length:** 22–30s

First-person, face on camera, the psychology of not shipping. The only
format where the *speaker* matters more than the information.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3s | A confession or an uncomfortably specific observation. "I had 61 ideas in a Notion doc and zero URLs." |
| Recognition | 3–12s | The behaviour, described so precisely it feels like surveillance. The 47 tabs. The domain bought and never used. |
| Reframe | 12–22s | The diagnosis — not laziness, a scoping error / a decision problem. |
| Instruction | 22–28s | One thing to do tonight. Small enough to actually do. |

**Fails when:** it scolds. Recognition + a way out. Never shame without exit.

---

## 8. `workflow-demo` — CRAFT
**Job:** saves + credibility · **Length:** 30–45s (the exception)

Screen recording, real cursor, real output. No slides pretending to be a demo.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3s | The *finished* output first. Show the destination, then rewind. |
| Setup | 3–10s | The prompt or the blank file. Readable text — zoom in. |
| Work | 10–32s | Sped-up build, 2–3 narrated decision points. Show one thing going wrong. |
| Result | 32–40s | Live URL on screen. |
| Loop | 40–44s | Elapsed time stated. "Nineteen minutes." |

**Fails when:** it's too fast to read or too clean to believe. The moment
something breaks and gets fixed is the most valuable second in the video.

---

## 9. `boring-business` — PROOF
**Job:** reach into non-founder audiences · **Length:** 28–34s

Unglamorous, high-pain, low-competition verticals — zoning, recalls,
compliance, brake shops, tenant rights. Strong performer because it's
genuinely novel next to the AI-tool feed, and it reaches the trojan audiences
from §7.1 of the strategy doc.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–4s | The absurd status quo. "Contractors still fax this." |
| Money | 4–10s | The industry's size or what the manual process costs. |
| Why nobody built it | 10–18s | Because it's boring. Say it. |
| The build | 18–28s | Simpler than the AI ideas, and the customer actually pays. |
| Loop | 28–32s | "Boring is a moat." |

---

## 10. `business-model-map` — PROOF/VERDICT
**Job:** saves + sends · **Length:** 30–34s

One problem, several businesses. Highest-value format per minute of
production and a natural carousel companion.

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–4s | "One problem. Four businesses. Pick your risk level." |
| Map | 4–26s | 4 models × ~5s: tool / marketplace / service / data. Each with a price and a difficulty. |
| Pick | 26–32s | Which one you'd build and why. Take a position. |

## Carousel layouts

Existing layouts in `content/social/_layouts/`. All 8 slides.

| Layout | Pillar | Use for | Slide 1 must |
|---|---|---|---|
| `wmv-default-8slide` | any | General frameworks | State a claim, not a topic |
| `idea-spotlight-8slide` | PROOF | One idea, deep | Lead with the number |
| `pipeline-8slide` | CRAFT | Step-by-step process | Show the end state first |
| `receipt-8slide` | RECEIPTS | Shipped proof | Show the live URL |
| `stack-grid-8slide` | CRAFT | Tool comparisons | Name the decision, not the tools |
| `digest-8slide` | PROOF | Weekly idea roundup | Promise a count + a filter |

**Calendar `format` values that resolve to carousels** (rather than Reels):

| Calendar format | Layout | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `weekly-digest` | `digest-8slide` or `stack-grid-8slide` | Saturday slot. Sourced from an article. |
| `shipped-receipt` | `receipt-8slide` | RECEIPTS pillar. Requires a real live URL. |

**Carousel rules:**
- Slide 1 does a Reel hook's entire job. If it doesn't stop the scroll, the
  other 7 slides don't exist.
- Slides 2–7 must each be independently screenshot-able. People screenshot
  single slides; each one is a distribution unit.
- Slide 8 is the only CTA slide. One action.
- **Never** put the payoff on slide 8 — put it on slide 6, so slide 7 can
  extend it and slide 8 can convert. Withholding the payoff to the end
  reads as a bait sequence and kills saves.
- Text: ≤ 20 words per slide. If it needs more, it's an article.

## Hook bank

Openers proven to fit this persona. Rotate; never reuse within 3 weeks.

**Pain-first** — "You've had this idea for 14 months." · "Your Notion doc has
61 ideas and zero URLs." · "You don't need another tutorial."

**Number-first** — "$4,200/mo from 84 customers." · "Eight hours. Three
screens. One weekend." · "This costs $12/month to run."

**Verdict-first** — "This idea gets posted every week and it doesn't work." ·
"Stop building for founders." · "Boring is a moat."

**Incumbent-first** — "[Tool] has 40M users and serves none of them well." ·
"Someone's charging $89/mo for a worse version of this."

**Question-first (comments)** — "Which one would you actually build?" ·
"Wrapper or platform?" · "Would you pay $29 for this?"
