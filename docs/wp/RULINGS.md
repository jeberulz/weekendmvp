| 2026-08-06 | WP27 / preview watermark contrast | The `S6` live axe pass reports one WP27-owned WCAG 2.1 AA violation on every template at every width: the decorative preview watermark, colour contrast **1.09** against a 3:1 requirement for large text (`text-white/[0.06]` on black). Fix it, or accept it? | **Accepted as exempt, not suppressed.** WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.3 exempts text that is "pure decoration" — content serving only an aesthetic purpose, providing no information, and having no functionality. The watermark is `aria-hidden`, non-interactive, and duplicates nothing: the readable `PreviewNotice` ("This is a private preview. It expires in 7 days and is not published.") carries the information and passes contrast on its own. Rejected raising the opacity, which needs roughly `#767676` to reach 3:1 and produces a loud watermark over every preview; rejected re-rendering it as a background-image SVG purely to satisfy a scanner, which changes nothing a user experiences and rewrites part of the `S3` template suite. **The violation is deliberately NOT excluded from the scan** — it is reported every run, and the exemption is what is recorded. Because the exemption is only sound while all three decoration conditions hold, `S6` added a per-template test (`tests/security/wp27-templates.test.tsx`) asserting the mark stays `aria-hidden`, stays non-focusable and functionality-free, and never becomes the sole source of information. If the watermark ever gains information, focus, or AT exposure, those tests fail and the accepted violation becomes a real one again. | Owner |
| 2026-08-06 | WP27 / S5 read exclusivity | WP27-S5's written AC says a capability claimed by A "cannot be claimed **or read** by user B." | 2026-08-07 | WP28 / legacy host fallback | The pre-WP28 middleware classified hosts as apex, www, and *everything else*, and everything else fell through to the full application. | 2026-08-07 | WP28 / tenant behaviour when Convex is unreachable | Independent review confirmed the fail-open publish check served **200 with the full Weekend MVP marketing 404** (nav, footer, Starter Kit signup, cal.com) at *every* tenant hostname including unclaimed ones, whenever Convex was down or a request exceeded the 1500 ms timeout.
