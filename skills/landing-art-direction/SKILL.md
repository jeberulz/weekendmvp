# Give Leo an art direction he can render

Read the research note and the approved brand-kit version. For a new kit, compare two credible directions against the reader’s task. Use the vendored Pro Max candidates as starting points. For a landing draft, carry the approved direction, palette, shape, density, voice and pair forward. Route a proposed change to those decisions through the brand revision flow. [Candidate lookup](../../lib/workspace/crew/design/proMax.ts)

Set the real business name, sample headline, paragraph and CTA in the proposed display and body faces. Check wrap, available weights, legibility of numbers or product labels, and the smallest-screen reading experience. Use an allowed Google Fonts pair for the direction and verify the matching renderer entry. Name both families and explain the job each does. Inspect the family’s licence when introducing a face to the registry. [Pair and layout lists](../../lib/workspace/contracts/artifacts.ts) · [Self-hosted font loaders](../../components/tenant-site/fonts.ts)

Decide what one visual will teach. Brief its subject, action, medium, focal point, brand colours, negative space and safe mobile crop. Specify what the viewer should learn, a short text alternative and the source or rights status. Choose a registered Phosphor icon when it adds a distinct cue to a label. Record asset state as existing, proposed or generated and inspected. If an image is still a brief, give Leo a usable textual product moment or a type-led treatment allowed by this direction.

**Answer Leo in one short handoff.** Identify the kit revision, the viewer’s hesitation, the type pair, recommended hero and signup treatment, and why this choice answers that hesitation. Include the exact asset description or available project-owned asset reference, fallback and one thing to inspect on mobile. Keep asset-generation instructions outside visitor-facing copy.
