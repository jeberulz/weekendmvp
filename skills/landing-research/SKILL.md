# Research a founder landing page

Read the exact approved blueprint, brand kit if present, founder brief and any idea research available to this business. Record the source versions. Identify the visitor, the moment that brought them here, the decision the page asks of them and the evidence they would need to take it. Offer a design hypothesis when the evidence runs out.

Use first-party material for product capabilities and founder promises. Library research can supply audience language with its provenance. An own-idea blueprint can support a preview while demand is still unvalidated. Keep example wording, campaign goals and buyer research distinct from customer testimonials. Surface a conflict between the approved brief and later notes for the lead.

When researching with an authorised browser, inspect references for a specific question. A specimen sheet can show how a reader compares two type choices. A booking page can show how to explain a commitment before asking for an email. Record the relevant principle in your own words and link its source. Preserve the brand’s own layout, copy and imagery. Hosted jobs consume the supplied notes and vendored candidates through the structured gateway.

**Hand Iris and Leo** a short note with the audience and trigger, the promise and its source, one likely hesitation, a concrete product example, and the intended action. For each influential reference record its owner, URL or internal document/version, observation and the design choice it supports. Mark any inference. Stop when the notes distinguish two credible design directions and give a reason to choose one.
