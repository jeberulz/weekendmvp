# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Weekend MVP serves indie founders, solo operators, and small teams deciding what to build and moving an evidence-backed idea toward a real launch. In the signed-in product they need to scan opportunities, preserve intent, understand the next safe action, and operate projects without becoming workflow or AI tooling experts.

## Product Purpose

Weekend MVP is a validation-first build platform. It connects the existing public research library to a private workspace where a user can save or pursue an idea, generate a concrete preview or Validation Report, confirm a brief, pay for a defined deliverable, publish a landing page, and track the resulting artifacts, tasks, leads, and credits. Success means users can move from evidence to a shipped, inspectable outcome without hidden automation, unclear spend, or a duplicate research corpus.

## Brand Personality

Calm, editorial, evidence-led.

The product should feel assured and useful: concise language, clear hierarchy, restrained color, and visible system status. It may borrow the composed workspace patterns of Hilos and the discovery utility of IdeaBrowser, but it must remain recognizably Weekend MVP rather than visually cloning either product.

## Anti-references

- Not an autonomous “AI cofounder” chat that invents or executes unbounded work.
- Not Polsia’s activity-feed spectacle, opaque credit burn, or build-before-validation model.
- Not a generic AI dashboard of purple gradients, glass panels, fake precision, or decorative motion.
- Not a wall of identical cards, nested panels, or unexplained metrics.
- Not a second private copy of public idea research; canonical research remains on the public idea page.
- Not an admin/support backdoor into another customer’s private data.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence before action.** Show the research, artifact, cost, and consequence before asking the user to proceed.
2. **One clear next move.** Every screen should make the highest-value supported action obvious without disguising a free-form agent behind a composer.
3. **Calm operational density.** Use a narrow rail, contextual navigation, strong typography, sparse dividers, and purposeful status treatment so the workspace is informative without feeling busy.
4. **Public truth, private state.** Reuse canonical idea records while keeping saved, interested, building, project, billing, and artifact state owner-scoped and non-indexable.
5. **Server-confirmed outcomes.** Money, credits, task completion, refunds, and publishing appear final only after authoritative server confirmation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA is the minimum. Keyboard focus must remain visible and logical; navigation regions and the single page `main` must be labelled semantically; color cannot be the only status signal; async changes use appropriate live regions without stealing focus; forms expose labels and linked errors; mobile navigation and sheets manage focus correctly; motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; and text/control contrast must meet AA in every state.
