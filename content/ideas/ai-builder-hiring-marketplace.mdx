## The Problem

Small-business owners watch competitors automate scheduling, follow-ups, and support. Hiring someone to build the same thing means Upwork profiles with unverifiable claims, Fiverr gigs that ship broken scripts, and self-proclaimed experts who take the money and vanish. An owner who wants automated appointment reminders posts a job, gets forty proposals, and picks on gut. The budget is there. Trust is the blocker.

r/smallbusiness (1.7M+) and Facebook groups like "AI strategy for entrepreneurs" show the same loop: owners want AI outcomes in plain English, not RFPs written for developers. Searches for "AI consultant for small business" have grown roughly 84% YoY. Boutique AI consulting firms exist, but discovery is cold LinkedIn outreach and $5K discovery calls. Generic marketplaces do not vet AI skills. Vertical SaaS embeds shallow AI that still needs someone to wire the messy local workflows.

The gap is a vetted marketplace where local service businesses (3–25 employees) hire screened AI implementers with escrow, standardized scopes, and milestone releases — without learning to write a technical brief.

## The Solution

Surepair (working name) is a marketplace connecting small local businesses to vetted AI builders. Implementers pass portfolio review, references, and a sample test before listing. Scoping templates turn "I need fewer no-shows" into a defined deliverable with milestones. Money sits in Stripe Connect escrow until each milestone ships.

Wedge: three productized jobs — scheduling automation, lead follow-up, and customer-support bots — for service businesses that already pay for tools but cannot wire them together.

**How it works:**

1. **Business intake** — Guided questionnaire maps outcomes (fewer no-shows, faster lead response) to a template scope with timeline and price band.
2. **Matched builders** — Platform suggests 2–3 vetted implementers with relevant tool experience (Calendly, HubSpot, OpenAI, Zapier/Make).
3. **Escrow kickoff** — Client funds first milestone; builder starts only after funds clear.
4. **Milestone delivery** — Shared dashboard; approve or request revision; release next tranche.
5. **Reputation loop** — Completion rate and satisfaction scores unlock better matching for both sides.

Weekend MVP: Airtable for matching, Stripe Connect for escrow, three scoping templates, ten hand-vetted builders, five pilot businesses.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Upwork** — Dominant freelance marketplace with escrow and dispute resolution. Client fees historically around ~10% range. No AI-specific vetting; overwhelming choice for non-technical owners.
- **Fiverr** — Productized gigs including chatbots and automation; seller fees often ~20%+. Cheap entry, weak for multi-week installs and ongoing support.
- **Toptal** — Heavily vetted experts at premium rates for startups and mid-market. Overkill for under-$20K local service projects.
- **Boutique AI consultancies** — High trust, high CAC via search/referrals; not a multi-vendor marketplace with escrow.

**Your Opportunity**

Own AI-specific vetting + plain-English scopes + escrow for the $2K–$25K local-service band. Position against Upwork as "safe AI installs," not "more freelancers." 10% take rate + $99/month priority matching.

## Business Model

- **Take rate** — 10% per completed project (primary).
- **Business subscription** — $99/month for priority matching and richer dashboards.
- **Premium support** — $1,000/year ongoing tuning retainers.
- **Implementer badge** — optional yearly verification fee.

**Unit Economics (illustrative)**

- Payment processing ~3% of GMV
- Path: 40 projects/month × $5K × 10% = $20K/month platform fees at early density

## Recommended Tech Stack

- **Next.js + Vercel** — public marketplace, dashboards.
- **Stripe Connect** — escrow-like milestone holds and payouts.
- **Postgres / Supabase** — users, projects, milestones, ratings.
- **Resend** — matching and milestone emails.
- **Airtable (MVP)** — manual vetting queue before full admin UI.

**2. Core Feature**

```text
Build the one feature that proves Surepair: a scoped project whose milestones are paid only when the owner approves them.
1. Seed three templates: scheduling_automation, lead_followup and support_bot. Each has questionnaire fields, default milestones and acceptance criteria written for non-technical owners.
2. /post-project picks a template, asks its questions, and creates a project with its default milestones.
3. When an implementer accepts the project, create a Stripe PaymentIntent for milestone 1 in test mode. The implementer starts only after it is funded.
4. When the owner approves a milestone, transfer its amount to the implementer and open the next milestone's payment. If a milestone fails its acceptance criteria twice, set the project to rematch.
5. Disputes freeze the project and stop all transfers.
Rules: money moves only on an approval or a dispute decision, never on a timer.
Empty state: an owner with no projects sees the template picker.
Done when: a test project runs through two milestones, a transfer happens only after approval, and a disputed project cannot release money.
```
