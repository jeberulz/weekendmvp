## The Problem

A creator with 200K followers gets a DM pitching a supplement line. Terms arrive over text. Equity stays verbal. Three months later the deal dies on promises. Another creator ships on a handshake and waits 90 days for a short payout. Each failed partnership makes the next cold outreach harder.

Influencer platforms (TikTok Creator Marketplace, Instagram, Aspire, CreatorIQ) optimize for short campaigns and media fees — not co-owned product lines with royalties, milestones, and escrowed payouts. Mid-tier creators (10K–500K) are too small for brand studios and too busy to learn supply chains. Operators who know DTC ops cannot systematically find trustworthy audiences. Reddit's r/influencermarketing (130K+) and Facebook collab groups complain about low pay, ghosting, and opaque terms.

The gap is structured deal flow: verified parties, written economics, tracked sales, and payouts against delivery.

## The Solution

Handsel is a partnership marketplace matching creators with verified brand operators/manufacturers. Creators list niche, audience size, and engagement. Operators pitch royalty splits, equity, and projections. Signed deals live on-platform with contracts, sale tracking, and milestone payouts so funds move on proof — not vibes.

Revenue: ~7% of gross sales through the platform plus $500–$2,000 premium matchmaking. Buyers are mid-tier creators who can sell out drops but cannot vet ops partners.

**How it works:**

1. **Dual onboarding** — Creators verify audience metrics; operators pass portfolio + reference checks.
2. **Structured pitches** — Operators submit templated terms (royalty %, equity, MOQ, timeline).
3. **Deal room** — E-sign agreement; escrow schedule tied to milestones (sample, PO, first ship).
4. **Sales tracking** — Shopify/Stripe hooks attribute revenue; platform computes payouts.
5. **Reputation graph** — Completion rate and on-time pay history unlock better matches.

Weekend MVP: Airtable + Stripe + DocuSign-style templates for 10 hand-matched deals in one vertical (e.g., supplements or apparel).

## Competitive Landscape

- **TikTok / Meta Creator Marketplaces** — Free matching for campaigns; platform lock-in; not built for equity/royalty product ops.
- **Aspire / CreatorIQ / Upfluence** — Enterprise SaaS (often multi-thousand $/month). Campaign analytics; weak product P&L tooling for mid-tier.
- **Brand collab apps** — Low-friction mobile matching; weak vetting and complex deal structures.
- **Shopify + multi-vendor tools** — Commerce rails after a partner exists; do not solve matchmaking or escrowed terms.

**Your Opportunity**

Be the co-founder marketplace for creators: 7% of attributed sales + premium match fees. Start in one category, publish deal templates, and make payout history the moat.

## Business Model

- **Take rate** — ~7% of gross sales on platform-tracked deals.
- **Premium matchmaking** — $500–$2,000 for curated intros.
- **Verified operator badge** — ~$500/year.
- **Analytics subscription** — $50–$200/month.

**Unit Economics (illustrative)**

- Payment + infra under 1% of GMV
- Path: 100 active deals × $8K/month GMV × 7% ≈ $56K/month at early density

- **Stripe Connect** — milestone payouts.
- **Shopify Admin API** — sales attribution.
- **PandaDoc / HelloSign** — contracts.
- **PostHog** — funnel analytics.

**2. Core Feature**

```text
Build the one feature that proves Handsel: a pitch that becomes a deal whose milestones both sides confirm, and a reputation that follows.
1. An operator submits a pitch to a creator with royalty percent, equity percent, minimum order quantity and timeline in weeks. The creator accepts or rejects it. Accepting creates a deal as draft.
2. A deal gets three milestones: sample_approved, po_placed and first_shipment. Each has two confirm buttons, one for each party. A milestone is done only when both have confirmed. When all three are done and a signed PDF is uploaded, the deal becomes completed.
3. After completion, each party leaves a 1 to 5 score for reliability and communication, once per deal.
4. A profile page shows the average scores, the number of completed deals and verified_at as a badge. Show "No deals yet" for a new profile.
5. Either party can set payout_disputed on a deal. Block an operator from sending new pitches when more than 10 percent of their deals are disputed, and tell them why.
Rules: only the two parties can read or change a deal, its milestones and its PDF. Text says plainly that Handsel holds no money and gives no legal advice.
Empty state: an empty directory says no one matches yet and links to the pitch form.
Done when: two test accounts take one pitch through accept, three confirmed milestones, a PDF and both reviews, the scores show on both profiles, and a third account cannot read the deal.
```

Trust is the scarce resource. Creators remember the brand that paid late; operators remember the creator who ghosted after samples. A reputation graph with on-time payout rates, sample-to-PO conversion, and completed deal counts becomes the moat that cold DMs cannot copy. Publish anonymized deal comps ("typical royalty for beauty creators with 100K–250K is X–Y%") so both sides arrive with realistic expectations and fewer dead pitches.

Legal templates matter more than fancy matching AI at the start. Ship three agreement flavors: royalty-only, royalty + equity, and white-label manufacturing with volume commitments. Use e-sign and store the executed PDF with the deal record. Stripe Connect milestone releases tied to sample approval, purchase order, and first shipment keep money moving with the work.

Go-to-market: Instagram creator-economy newsletters, r/influencermarketing AMAs with a free "deal checklist" lead magnet, and outbound to 50 mid-tier supplement and apparel operators who already sell DTC but lack creator co-brands. Hand-match the first ten deals; instrument every step; only automate matching after you know which pitch fields predict acceptance. The weekend MVP is a Notion + Stripe + DocuSign stack with a thin Next.js shell — density of closed deals beats feature count.


Category density beats horizontal scale early. Win beauty or apparel first so operators refer each other and creators see familiar brand names. Cross-category marketplace liquidity is a myth at seed stage — five closed beauty deals in one city teach more than fifty empty profiles across six niches. Publish a monthly "partnership index" with anonymized royalty bands; that content becomes inbound for both sides and compounds SEO around "creator brand partnership deal."
