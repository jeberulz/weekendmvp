## The Problem

Small-business websites are lawsuit magnets waiting to happen. One ADA Title III complaint and an owner faces five-figure legal fees plus the reputation hit of looking indifferent to disabled users. Most owners know they "should" be compliant. Almost none know what is actually broken on their Squarespace, WordPress, or Shopify site — and enterprise tools assume a full-time developer who speaks WCAG.

ADA-related digital lawsuits have surged for years. The digital accessibility software market was about **USD 721M in 2023** and is projected toward **USD 1.3B by 2030** (~9% CAGR) ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/digital-accessibility-software-market-report)). Leaders like accessiBe and UserWay sell overlays criticized in developer communities; AudioEye and Level Access price for enterprises. Free tools like WAVE and axe help engineers but do not give owners a plain-English fix list. r/smallbusiness and r/webdev threads are full of "am I going to get sued?" anxiety without a $29/month answer.

## The Solution

AccessCheck is an AI-powered scanner that crawls a small-business site, flags accessibility issues, and returns a fix-it checklist in plain English — not WCAG jargon. Owners scan, see what is broken, and follow step-by-step fixes. Optional WordPress/Shopify guidance and agency white-label reports expand the wedge from fear to ongoing monitoring.

**How it works:**

1. **Paste URL** — Crawl homepage and key templates; run axe-core / WAVE-class rules plus AI explanations.
2. **Plain-English report** — Each issue explains who it hurts and how to fix it on common CMSes.
3. **Priority score** — Rank by legal risk and effort so owners knock out high-impact items first.
4. **Re-scan** — After fixes, verify clearance; email alerts when new issues appear.
5. **Share / white-label** — Agencies export branded PDFs for client retainers.

Weekend MVP: URL → Playwright crawl → axe-core → Claude explanations → PDF report. Stripe $29/month. WordPress plugin later.
