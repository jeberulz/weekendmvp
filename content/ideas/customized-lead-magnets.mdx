## The Problem

Most "lead magnets" are a recycled PDF with the founder's face on page one. The form is a Mailchimp embed. The promise is generic. ConvertKit and HubSpot will host it. Canva will make it pretty. None of them assemble a magnet that matches the visitor's job, tool stack, and the page they came from.

Marketers know this. r/Emailmarketing (80k+) and r/marketing (1.8 million) are a graveyard of "my lead magnet converts at 0.8%." Klaviyo's Facebook group (64k) is the same complaint with better merch. Hormozi's YouTube (hundreds of thousands per upload) sold everyone on the value ladder and then left them with Canva.

HubSpot from $50/month is a CRM that happens to have forms. Canva Pro at $12.99/month is a design tool that happens to export PDFs. Pathmonk will personalize B2B magnets — price unspecified in the Ideabrowser cut — but they are not a weekend-MVP you can clone for creators.

The other failure mode is the opposite: a 40-page "ultimate guide" nobody finishes. Opt-in looks fine in Klaviyo. Sales calls start with "I haven't read it yet." A customized magnet is shorter on purpose — a 4-page checklist that names *their* stack — so the email sequence can assume they did the work.

## The Solution

A magnet builder that starts from the landing-page offer and the visitor segment, then emits a unique PDF / quiz / checklist. $49 DIY one-time or $99/month Pro in the ladder. Free templates are the wedge. The product is "this visitor got a different magnet than the last one."

**How it works:**

1. **Offer lock** — Paste the landing page or describe the offer, ICP, and the one transformation. The app refuses to generate until those three fields exist.
2. **Segment hooks** — 3–5 segments (role, tool, pain). Each gets a title, outline, and CTA that still sells the same backend.
3. **Ship** — Generate the assets, host the form, drop the embed. $20 analytics add-on shows which segment magnet actually opted in.

## Competitive Landscape

- **HubSpot** — from $50/month. $1.73B (2024). Forms + CRM. The magnet is still a static file you uploaded.
- **Canva** — Pro $12.99/month, 125M users. Makes the PDF. Does not route by segment.
- **Mailchimp / ConvertKit (Kit)** — form + email. No asset engine.
- **Pathmonk** — AI-personalized B2B magnets. Closest product. Price not in the Ideabrowser extract. Enterprise motion.
- **DIY GPT + Canva** — the real substitute. Slow, untracked, ugly UTM.

**Your Opportunity**

Free template pack (email) → $49 DIY (one magnet, three segments, you export) → $99/month Pro (hosted forms, variants, swap without republishing) → $20 analytics add-on → $10k+/year if an agency wants 25 clients on one invoice. Pathmonk can have B2B sales teams. You take creators and small info products who already live in Kit and Klaviyo.

Ship the embed so ripping you out hurts. DIY $49 is a Twitter impulse. Pro $99 is the business because the form lives on your origin. If they export a ZIP they will not come back.

## Recommended Tech Stack

- **App:** Next.js 15 on Vercel
- **Auth / billing:** Clerk + Stripe
- **DB:** Supabase — `offers`, `segments`, `magnets`, `submissions`
- **Gen:** Claude for outline + copy; a PDF renderer (react-pdf) not a screenshot
- **Forms:** your embed + a native Kit / Klaviyo webhook
- **Analytics:** PostHog or a thin events table — $20 add-on is this query
