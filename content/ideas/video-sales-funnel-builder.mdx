## The Problem

Info-product and coaching sellers still buy a ClickFunnels template and a talking-head from Fiverr and call it a VSL. Agencies (Gisteo, Skyline) will build the real thing for $1,500–$8,000. ClickFunnels is $97–$297/month plus templates that look like 2017. Synthesia will give you an avatar. InVideo / Canva / Animoto will give you a slideshow. The missing SKU is a $499 done-in-a-week funnel that is actually a sales video with a page, not a page with a video slapped on it.

The "3x better than landing pages" line is category folklore — ClickFunnels and the VSL listicle industrial complex repeat it. Use it as positioning, then measure your own close rate. Do not print it as a peer-reviewed fact.

The buyer is a solo founder who already has a $99–$2,000 offer and a Loom that is too long. They will not pay $6,000. They will pay $499 if the page is live on Friday.

The other buyer is the course creator who already has ClickFunnels and a 42-minute "VSL" that is a webinar with the chat cut out. They do not need another page builder. They need someone to force an 8–12 minute structure and a checkout that does not open a new tab. That is editorial work wearing a product costume. Charge $499 for the costume. Do the editorial.

## The Solution

A VSL sprint: script, edit, page, checkout. $499 one-time (the product title). The Ideabrowser ladder also has a $500 Starter, $1,000–$3,000/month retainers, $500–$2,000 analytics add-on, and a $10k–$20k VR/AR fantasy tier you should ignore in year one.

**How it works:**

1. **Offer quiz** — Offer, price, avatar, objections, proof assets. The quiz refuses to start the script until price and the one transformation exist.
2. **Script + cut** — 8–12 minute structure (hook, problem, unique mechanism, proof, offer, FAQ, close). They record on Loom or upload. You cut, caption, and add pattern interrupts — not an avatar unless they ask.
3. **Page + checkout** — One long page, VSL above the fold, checkout in-page. Stripe. Analytics events on play, 25%, 50%, 75%, offer click. Optional $500+/month to refresh the cut when the offer changes.

## Market Research

Sales-funnel software: $80.31 billion in 2024 to $142.96 billion by 2032 (10.1% CAGR) in the Ideabrowser pack. A narrower "digital marketing funnel" cut cites $2.5 billion by 2025 at 22% CAGR — two TAMs, do not mash them. Your SAM is founders who would have hired Gisteo and cannot.

Campaign Refinery, WPFunnels, ClickFunnels' "DNA of a high-converting VSL," Gisteo, and Skyline Social are the citation set. They agree on structure more than on price. The hole is the $300–$2,000 band: DIY below, agencies above.

Why the 3x claim persists: a watched sales argument beats a scanned landing page on complex offers. That is not magic. It is time-on-page plus a human face. If your $499 sprint ships a 4-minute slideshow with stock B-roll, you are InVideo with a higher invoice. The cut has to be their face, their proof, their price.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Gisteo** — $2,000–$8,000 per project. Real VSL shop. You are the cheaper sibling, not the clone.
- **Skyline Social** — $1,500–$6,000 per funnel. Same band.
- **ClickFunnels** — $97–$297/month. Page + template. The video is your problem.
- **Synthesia** — SaaS avatars. Fine for compliance training. Cold for a $2,000 cohort.
- **Fiverr / Upwork** — $50–$800 and a dice roll.
- **InVideo / Canva / Animoto** — DIY motion. No offer logic, no checkout.

**Your Opportunity**

Free VSL quiz (score + email) → $499 Starter sprint (script + cut + page, one offer) → $1,000–$3,000/month if they launch quarterly → $500–$2,000 analytics only if they already have traffic. Position $499 as the missing band. Do not become a ClickFunnels theme shop. The video is the product; the page is the wrapper.

Launch in one niche (B2B cohort coaches, or fitness challenges — pick one) so the script template is not generic. A verticalized $499 beats a horizontal "any VSL" every time. Gisteo can keep the $8,000 custom jobs.

Ignore the VR/AR $10k–$20k ladder rung until someone asks.

## Recommended Tech Stack

- **App:** Next.js 15 on Vercel
- **Auth / billing:** Clerk + Stripe Checkout ($499) + Stripe Billing (retainer)
- **Video:** Mux (player + quartile events)
- **DB:** Supabase — `offers`, `scripts`, `funnels`, `events`
- **Page:** a single funnel template, not a builder — you edit, they do not
- **LLM:** Claude for the script from the quiz
- **Edit:** CapCut / Descript in v1; do not build an NLE
