## The Problem

Info-product and coaching sellers still buy a ClickFunnels template and a talking-head from Fiverr and call it a VSL. Agencies (Gisteo, Skyline) will build the real thing for $1,500–$8,000. ClickFunnels is $97–$297/month plus templates that look like 2017. Synthesia will give you an avatar. InVideo / Canva / Animoto will give you a slideshow. The missing SKU is a $499 done-in-a-week funnel that is actually a sales video with a page, not a page with a video slapped on it.

The "3x better than landing pages" line is category folklore — ClickFunnels and the VSL listicle industrial complex repeat it. Use it as positioning, then measure your own close rate. Do not print it as a peer-reviewed fact.

The buyer is a solo founder who already has a $99–$2,000 offer and a Loom that is too long. They will not pay $6,000. They will pay $499 if the page is live on Friday.

The other buyer is the course creator who already has ClickFunnels and a 42-minute "VSL" that is a webinar with the chat cut out. They do not need another page builder. They need someone to force an 8–12 minute structure and a checkout that does not open a new tab. That is editorial work wearing a product costume. Charge $499 for the costume. Do the editorial.

## The Solution

A VSL sprint: script, edit, page, checkout. $499 one-time (the product title). The Ideabrowser ladder also has a $500 Starter, $1,000–$3,000/month retainers, $500–$2,000 analytics add-on, and a $10k–$20k VR/AR fantasy tier you should ignore in year one.

**How it works:**

1. **Offer quiz** — Offer, price, avatar, objections, proof assets. The quiz refuses to start the script until price and the one transformation exist.
2. **Script + cut** — 8–12 minute structure (hook, problem, unique mechanism, proof, offer, FAQ, close). They record on Loom or upload. You cut, caption, and add pattern interrupts — not an avatar unless they ask.
3. **Page + checkout** — One long page, VSL above the fold, checkout in-page. Stripe. Analytics events on play, 25%, 50%, 75%, offer click. Optional $500+/month to refresh the cut when the offer changes.

## Market Research

Sales-funnel software: $80.31 billion in 2024 to $142.96 billion by 2032 (10.1% CAGR) in the Ideabrowser pack. A narrower "digital marketing funnel" cut cites $2.5 billion by 2025 at 22% CAGR — two TAMs, do not mash them. Your SAM is founders who would have hired Gisteo and cannot.

Campaign Refinery, WPFunnels, ClickFunnels' "DNA of a high-converting VSL," Gisteo, and Skyline Social are the citation set. They agree on structure more than on price. The hole is the $300–$2,000 band: DIY below, agencies above.

Why the 3x claim persists: a watched sales argument beats a scanned landing page on complex offers. That is not magic. It is time-on-page plus a human face. If your $499 sprint ships a 4-minute slideshow with stock B-roll, you are InVideo with a higher invoice. The cut has to be their face, their proof, their price.

**Your Opportunity**

Free VSL quiz (score + email) → $499 Starter sprint (script + cut + page, one offer) → $1,000–$3,000/month if they launch quarterly → $500–$2,000 analytics only if they already have traffic. Position $499 as the missing band. Do not become a ClickFunnels theme shop. The video is the product; the page is the wrapper.

Launch in one niche (B2B cohort coaches, or fitness challenges — pick one) so the script template is not generic. A verticalized $499 beats a horizontal "any VSL" every time. Gisteo can keep the $8,000 custom jobs.

## Recommended Tech Stack

- **App:** Next.js 15 on Vercel
- **Auth / billing:** Clerk + Stripe Checkout ($499) + Stripe Billing (retainer)
- **Video:** Mux (player + quartile events)
- **DB:** Supabase — `offers`, `scripts`, `funnels`, `events`
- **Page:** a single funnel template, not a builder — you edit, they do not
- **LLM:** Claude for the script from the quiz
- **Edit:** CapCut / Descript in v1; do not build an NLE

## AI Prompts to Build This

Copy and paste these into Claude, Cursor, or your favorite AI tool.

**1. Project Setup**

```text
Build the weekend MVP of the "Video Sales Funnel Builder": an owner answers an offer quiz and gets an 8 to 12 minute script outline and a recording checklist, then publishes one long page with their video above the fold and a button to their own checkout. The page records how far people watch.

Stack: Next.js (App Router, TypeScript), Tailwind, Supabase (Postgres, Row Level Security, Auth with email magic link), Mux for video playback and its player events, the Anthropic API for the outline. The funnel is one fixed template, not a page builder. Deploy on Vercel.

Tables (Row Level Security on, an owner reads only their own rows; the public page and its events go through server routes):
- offers(id, user_id, name, price_text, avatar, transformation, objections jsonb, proof_assets jsonb, locked_at)
- scripts(id, offer_id, outline jsonb, checklist jsonb, created_at)
- funnels(id, offer_id, slug, mux_playback_id, cta_url, bullets jsonb, faq jsonb, status)
- events(id, funnel_id, kind, visitor_hash, created_at)  kind is view, play, q25, q50, q75, complete or cta_click

Screens: /login, /offers/new (the quiz), /offers/[id] (the outline and the checklist), /funnels/[id] (publish and stats), /f/[slug] (the public page).
Env vars (names only): NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL, NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY, SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY (server only), ANTHROPIC_API_KEY.

Do not build: billing, sprint or retainer products, a payment integration, a drag-and-drop page builder, a video editor, an analytics add-on.
Done when: npm run dev starts, you can sign in, and the four tables exist with Row Level Security on.
```

**2. Core Feature**

```text
Build the one feature that proves the Funnel Builder: a script plan made from the owner's own facts, and a drop-off view that shows where people leave.
1. The quiz asks for the offer, the price, the ideal customer, the transformation, the objections and the proof the owner really has. Refuse to generate until the price and the transformation exist, and set locked_at.
2. Ask the Anthropic API for an outline as JSON { sections: [{ name, minutes, talking_points: string[] }] } with the sections hook, problem, mechanism, proof, offer, frequently asked questions and close. The prompt says to use only the owner's answers and never to invent a statistic, a testimonial or a result.
3. Check in code that the minutes add up to between 8 and 12 and that every number or name in the proof section appears in the owner's proof assets. Drop anything that does not.
4. Show the outline with a recording checklist (framing, proof on screen, no long ramble) and a script length that ends inside the time budget.
5. The owner records elsewhere, uploads to Mux and pastes the playback id and the address of their own checkout page (https only). Publish the funnel at /f/[slug], server-rendered, with the video above the fold, the bullets, the frequently asked questions and the button.
6. Record view, play, 25, 50 and 75 percent, complete and button clicks from the Mux player and the page, with a visitor hash that rotates daily and uses no cookie.
7. The stats page shows the drop-off from each step to the next and marks any step where more than half of the people before it leave, with the script section that sits there.
Rules: never promise a conversion lift or a rate. Show the number of viewers behind every percentage and hide a percentage under 30 viewers.
Empty state: before the quiz, show the quiz and what a good proof asset looks like.
Done when: generation refuses without a price and a transformation, a proof line with an invented number is dropped, the outline fits 8 to 12 minutes, a published page plays and logs the quartiles and the stats flag a section with more than half dropping.
```

**3. Landing Page**

```text
Build a one-page landing site for the Video Sales Funnel Builder, a script and a page for your offer video.
Hero: "A video sales page that says what you actually sell." Sub: "Answer a short quiz about your offer. Get a script outline and a recording checklist, then publish one clean page and see where people stop watching." One button: Join the waitlist.
Sections: a sample outline with its time budget, how it works in four steps (quiz, outline, record, publish), a sample drop-off view with one flagged section, and an FAQ on what is generated (only from your answers), where checkout happens (your own page) and what is not promised (any conversion result).
Waitlist: store the email in a waitlist table in Supabase. No other service.
Style: Geist, a warm off-white background, near-black type, one amber accent.
Done when: the page renders on a phone and a submitted email appears in the waitlist table.
```

```text
Use a design or image tool for this one. A coding agent cannot draw a logo.
Brand for the Video Sales Funnel Builder: pick a short working name, then design a wordmark and an icon that suggest a play button with a funnel narrowing beneath it.
Colors: warm off-white, near-black and one amber. Type: Geist, with tabular numbers for percentages.
Deliverables: wordmark, icon, a drop-off bar style that marks a flagged step by shape as well as color, a script outline layout, and one launch graphic.
Done when: each deliverable is saved in one folder and the flagged step is distinguishable without color.
```
