## The Problem

A founder ships 80 percent of a Bubble CRM over a weekend, then the Stripe plugin loops charges, a Make scenario silently drops records, or a Webflow CMS collection refuses to bind. The pitch of no-code was self-sufficiency. The last 20 percent is a specialist job: a specific plugin, a specific interaction, a specific API. That founder does not need a six-week agency rebuild. They need someone who has already fixed this exact break, by Friday.

Current discovery is backwards. Upwork and Fiverr bury Bubble and Webflow under generalist web-dev categories; you browse profiles, decode portfolios, and hope the person who says “Bubble expert” has actually shipped the plugin you are stuck on. Bubble’s certified developer directory and Webflow’s Expert list are more qualified — and still profile-first. You search people, then explain the ticket. Reddit’s r/nocode (123K+ members, 130-plus comments on automation threads) and r/Bubbleio (9.5K) plus Facebook’s Bubble.io Community (26.3K) will often solve it for free, if you wait, if a stranger feels like helping, and if you are fine posting production bugs in public. None of those channels hold funds, score completion, or let a specialist claim a ticket without a sales call.

The quote that should be on the landing page is already in the research: “Finding someone who knows the specific Bubble plugin or Webflow interaction you need requires browsing profiles, not posting a task.” That search tax is the product. Citizen developers now outnumber professional developers 4 to 1 at large enterprises, and 70 percent of new enterprise applications are projected to use no-code or low-code by 2026. The people hitting the wall are not hobbyists. They are operators with a live app and a broken payment flow. A marketplace organized by the problem, not the person, is the only matching model that respects how the break actually presents.

## The Solution

TaskPatch is a bounty board for no-code breaks. The builder posts the platform, the symptom, and a fixed price. Specialists filter by Bubble, Webflow, Make, Zapier, or Airtable and by completion history, then claim the ticket. Funds sit in Stripe Connect escrow until the poster marks the fix complete. Every specialist carries a speed-and-accuracy score. Matching runs on the problem, not a profile gallery.

Do not open the board empty. Onboard ten verified specialists across Bubble, Webflow, and Make first. Run the first 50 tasks by hand through Airtable so you learn which descriptions convert, which price bands get claimed inside a day, and where disputes cluster. Automate routing only after you have a pattern library. Premium placement ($50–$200) buys same-day visibility. A $99/month builder plan shortens the claim window. The take is 15 percent on completed tasks.

The surface is deliberately small. No proposals, no hourly retainers, no “tell me about your project.” A ticket has a title, a platform tag, a short repro, a budget, and a deadline. Specialists who have closed 40 Stripe-on-Bubble tickets see that listing and claim it. You are selling a claimed ticket, not a freelancer relationship.

**How it works:**

1. **Post the break** — Builder picks platform (Bubble, Webflow, Make, Zapier, Airtable), writes the symptom and a fixed price, and funds escrow. Optional $50–$200 bump for same-day placement.
2. **Claim** — Verified specialists filter by platform and score; one claim locks the ticket. Poster sees the specialist’s completion history before the lock, not a 40-proposal inbox.
3. **Fix in escrow** — Work happens off-platform (in the builder’s app). Poster confirms or opens a dispute. Funds release on confirm; a thin admin queue handles the rest.
4. **Score and reuse** — Speed and accuracy update the specialist’s rank. Repeat posters get a $99/month priority window so the next break does not go back to Reddit.

## Competitive Landscape

Every incumbent is either a generalist marketplace, a platform directory, or an unpaid forum. None sell a claimed ticket with platform filters.

- **Upwork** — Default hire path. Escrow, disputes, brand trust, millions of freelancers. No-code is a buried category; matching optimizes across all verticals, not “Bubble Stripe plugin.” Client/freelancer fees historically **5–20 percent**. You will lose on catalog size. You win on time-to-claim for a named break.
- **Fiverr** — Productized gigs, fast checkout, **$5–$500+** packages, seller fees often around **20 percent**. Fine for a logo. Weak for a production Make scenario that is dropping rows. The gig card is the opposite of a repro: it sells a package, not a diagnosis.
- **Bubble Certified Developer Directory / Webflow Experts** — Real platform skill, endorsement, $50–$250+/hour typical. Still browse-then-email. No task post, no cross-platform ticket (Bubble + Zapier + Airtable), no standardized escrow, no completion score you can sort.
- **No-code Slack, Discord, Facebook, Reddit** — Fast, free, high-trust peers. Zero SLA, zero payment, specialists have no reason to prioritize a stranger. This is your unpaid competitor and your specialist-recruiting channel.
- **Full-service no-code agencies** — $5K–$50K projects, high trust, wrong unit of work. They will not bid $350 Stripe-loop tickets.
- **AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, in-editor copilots)** — Eat “where is this setting” tickets. They will not debug a live Stripe webhook loop against a specific Bubble plugin version. Position above DIY and below agencies.

**Your Opportunity**

Own the $200–$1,000 platform-specific repair. Incumbents will not drop to that SKU without cannibalizing hourly and gig economics. Three things they will not copy quickly: (1) problem-first posting instead of profile search, (2) platform + plugin tags with completion history, (3) a specialist roster you actually vet. Seed ten named experts, run 50 manual jobs, then turn on the board. Course creators and no-code educators are the distribution: they already produce the students who hit the wall.

## Business Model

Marketplace take rate plus a thin SaaS layer on the poster side. Do not invent a fourth pricing axis.

- **Free post** ($0) — First ticket free of take, or take waived on the first completed job. Cold-start bait for posters and a sample for specialists.
- **Take rate** (15 percent) — Primary revenue. Applied on successful completion only. Aligns you with closed tickets, not listings.
- **Priority matching** ($99/month) — Faster claim window, bump credits, saved platform filters. For operators who break things weekly.
- **Premium placement** ($50–$200 per task) — Same-day visibility. Frontend cash without changing the take.
- **AI / integration surcharge** (20 percent take on tagged AI tickets) — Later. OpenAI-in-Bubble work prices higher and specialists are scarce.
- **Backend** — Agency white-label ($500/month + 10 percent) and a $10K+/year custom program once you have volume. Not week one.

Path to $10K/month: roughly 220 completed tasks at $300 average × 15 percent ≈ $9.9K, or 150 tasks plus ~40 Priority subs. At 500 tasks/month the take alone is $22.5K. Stripe Connect plus modest LLM routing on descriptions is well under $1 per closed job if you keep matching rules-based at first.

**Unit Economics**

- **$45–$75** — Gross take per $300–$500 task at 15 percent
- **under $1** — Variable cost per closed job (Connect + email + light LLM)
- **~$80 percent** — Gross margin after processing
- **$50–$100** — Target CAC per poster (communities first; paid search later at those $8–$12 CPCs)
- **~$115** — Illustrative 12-month LTV on a Priority poster who drops two tickets a month

## Recommended Tech Stack

This is a two-sided board with escrow, not a social network. Optimize for ticket state machine, Connect payouts, and a specialist claim lock. Hand-match in Airtable until the 50th job.

- **Next.js 14 + Vercel** — Public board, poster dashboard, specialist inbox. App Router. No separate marketing site until the board converts.
- **Supabase (Auth + Postgres)** — Tables: users (role poster or specialist), platforms, tickets (status open/claimed/in_progress/complete/disputed), claims, reviews, escrow_intents. RLS so posters see their tickets and specialists see open + their claims.
- **Stripe Connect** — Destination charges, hold then transfer on confirm. Do not write custom escrow. Validate Connect before you write a second feature.
- **Airtable (week-one ops)** — Specialist CRM, the first 50 jobs, dispute notes. Sync to Postgres when the pattern is boring.
- **Resend** — Claim, confirm, and dispute mail. Most of the product is email.
- **Claude (optional, thin)** — Rewrite a messy ticket into platform + symptom + acceptance checks. Do not auto-assign in v1.
- **Clerk or Supabase Auth** — Magic link. These users do not want another password.

**1. Project Setup**

```text
Create a Next.js 14 (App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind) app called TaskPatch: a specialist repair marketplace for no-code builders.

Roles: poster, specialist, admin.

Postgres (Supabase) tables:
- users (id, role, email, stripe_account_id nullable)
- specialist_profiles (user_id, platforms text[], bio, completed_count, avg_hours_to_close, accuracy_score, verified boolean)
- tickets (id, poster_id, platform enum bubble|webflow|make|zapier|airtable, title, repro, budget_cents, bump_cents default 0, status enum open|claimed|in_progress|complete|disputed|cancelled, specialist_id nullable, created_at)
- claims (id, ticket_id, specialist_id, claimed_at) unique on ticket_id where status is claimed or later
- reviews (ticket_id, rating 1-5, hours_to_close, note)
- escrow_events (ticket_id, stripe_payment_intent_id, stripe_transfer_id, state held|released|refunded)

Stripe Connect: poster pays budget_cents + optional bump into a PaymentIntent; on poster confirm, transfer 85 percent to the specialist Connect account and keep 15 percent application fee. Priority plan is a $99/month Stripe Price.

Auth via Supabase magic link. RLS: posters CRUD own tickets; specialists read open tickets plus their claimed rows; admin bypass.

Env: NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL, SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY, STRIPE_SECRET_KEY, STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET, RESEND_API_KEY.
```

**2. Ticket Board + Claim Lock**

```text
Build the TaskPatch board.

Public /board lists open tickets with platform badge, budget, age, bump flag. Filter by platform. Sort: bumped first, then newest.

POST /tickets creates a ticket only after Stripe PaymentIntent succeeds for budget_cents + bump_cents. Default take 15 percent stored as application_fee_amount.

POST /tickets/:id/claim: if status is open, lock specialist_id, set claimed, insert claims row, email both parties via Resend. Reject if already claimed (unique constraint). Poster can view the specialist profile (platforms, completed_count, accuracy_score) before the lock UI confirms.

Specialist /inbox shows claimed + in_progress. Poster /tickets/:id has Confirm fix (releases transfer) and Open dispute (flags admin). Admin /disputes is a table, not a workflow engine.

Do not build proposals, chat, or hourly tracking. One claim per ticket.
```
