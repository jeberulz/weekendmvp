## The Short Answer

**If you build it yourself with AI tools in 2026: roughly $200–$2,000 in cash to launch, then about $30–$120/month to run while you're small.**

**If you hire: $3,000–$15,000 freelance, or $15,000–$60,000 agency.**

Anyone quoting $80K for a "weekend MVP" is selling a finished product, not a minimum test.

That's the answer. The rest of this page is the math so you can pick a path without getting fleeced — or lying to yourself about "free."

Still choosing *what* to spend on? Browse scoped builds on [startup ideas](/startup-ideas) before you open Stripe for an agency deposit.

## What "MVP Cost" Actually Means

An MVP is not a smaller version of the final product. It's the cheapest experiment that proves someone will **pay** (or take a hard commitment).

So cost = cash tools + infra + **your time**. Ignore time and every AI-tool tweet looks like a free lunch.

## Path Comparison (2026)

| Path | Cash to launch | Typical time | When it's right |
|------|----------------|--------------|-----------------|
| Founder + Cursor / Claude Code | $200–$2,000 | 3–12 weeks evenings | You can specify clearly and review what AI ships |
| No-code (Bubble / Glide / FlutterFlow) | $300–$3,000 | 2–8 weeks | Forms + data + workflows, not custom systems |
| Freelancer | $3,000–$15,000 | 4–12 weeks | Validated demand, fixed scope, you can't build |
| Boutique agency | $15,000–$60,000 | 8–16 weeks | Compliance, enterprise buyers, hard deadline |
| AI prototype (Bolt / Lovable / v0) | $0–$500 | 1–2 weeks | Demo / waitlist proof — often not production |

Sources for these bands include 2026 founder cost breakdowns from [tinyctl](https://tinyctl.dev/tutorials/how-much-does-saas-mvp-cost-2026/) and vibe-coding budget writeups landing most lean stacks around **$66–$240/month** once you leave pure free tiers.

## The Monthly Bill Nobody Quotes

Cash to *build* is not cash to *run*.

Typical launch stack:

| Component | MVP range |
|-----------|-----------|
| Domain | $10–$50/year |
| Hosting (Vercel / similar) | $0–$20/mo |
| Database + auth (Supabase / Neon) | $0–$25/mo |
| Email (Resend / Loops) | $0–$15/mo |
| Analytics | $0–$30/mo |
| Error tracking | $0–$30/mo |
| AI coding tools (Cursor / Claude) | $20–$100/mo |
| LLM APIs *if users trigger models* | $0–$500+/mo |

Most non-AI products sit at **~$30–$50/month** at launch. One 2026 indie survey put the **median vibe-coded SaaS** around **$87/month**, with the top quartile near $340 — almost always because of user-facing LLM usage, not hosting.

If your product calls Claude or OpenAI on every click, model cost-per-user before you price. Pair this with [what it costs to run an AI app](/articles/what-it-costs-to-run-an-ai-app) and [how to price your weekend MVP](/articles/how-to-price-your-weekend-mvp).

## Worked Example: Solo Founder, AI-Assisted Web SaaS

**Assumptions:** one core workflow, Next.js, Supabase, Stripe, Vercel Hobby → Pro later.

**Month 0–1 (build):**

- Cursor Pro: $20
- Domain: $15
- Everything else: free tiers
- **Cash out:** ~$35–$80 (plus your nights/weekends)

**Month 2 (first users):**

- Still mostly free
- Stripe takes 2.9% + $0.30 when money moves
- Optional: Resend / Sentry paid when free limits bite

**Honest total to first paid customer:** usually **under $200 cash** if you stay ruthless on scope. The expensive line item is 80–150 hours of founder time.

## Where People Waste Money

1. **Agency before validation** — paying $25K to learn nobody cares
2. **Building auth, teams, admin, and five integrations** before one user finishes the core job
3. **Treating prototypes as products** — Bolt/Lovable demos still need auth, billing, and ops
4. **Ignoring LLM unit economics** — free during demo week, painful at 1,000 MAU
5. **Open-ended freelancer contracts** — "build my MVP" without a feature freeze becomes an agency project at freelancer rates

## The Decision Tree

1. Can you describe the product in one sentence **and** review AI-written code? → Solo AI-assisted path ($200–$2K).
2. Is it mostly forms + records + email? → No-code ($300–$3K).
3. Validated waitlist / prepayments, but you can't ship? → Freelancer with a fixed deliverable list ($3K–$15K).
4. Still guessing the problem? → Don't spend. Talk to customers first, or pick a sharper problem from [startup ideas](/startup-ideas).

## FAQ

### How much does an MVP cost if I'm non-technical?

Cash can still stay under a few thousand with no-code or AI builders — but budget for more time, more scrap-and-rebuild cycles, and a smaller feature set. Or hire after you have payment intent, not before.

### Is a $0 MVP real?

Almost. Free tiers cover hosting and DB for early traffic. You'll still pay a domain, and your time isn't free. "Zero" usually means "no invoice yet."

### Should I raise money to build the MVP?

Usually no. Raise to accelerate distribution after you have a working wedge and early revenue — not to fund a speculative build.
